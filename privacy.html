Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Signal Theory Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Signal Theory Network YouTube channel to publish its own videos.

What it accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth), the tool uploads videos and captions to the owner's own YouTube channel, reads the status and metadata of those videos, and reads the channel's own YouTube Analytics reports to improve future videos.

What it does not do

It does not access, collect, store, sell or share data about any other person or YouTube user. It does not post comments, send messages, or delete videos.

Storage and security

The authorization token is stored only as an encrypted secret in the owner's private repository and is used solely to perform the actions above. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The tool uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
